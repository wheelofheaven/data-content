description = "In Halbouty (ed.), Giant Oil and Gas Fields of the Decade 1990–1999, AAPG Memoir 78, pp. 15–105. The world's 877 giant fields cluster in 27 regions, about 30% of the land surface, and sort into six tectonic settings: continental passive margins fronting major ocean basins (304 giants), continental rifts and sag basins (271), terminal continental collisions (173), accretion-related collisions (71), strike-slip margins (50), and plain subduction margins (8). The mainstream tectonic account of the concentration Nehring mapped as the 'ring of oil'."
