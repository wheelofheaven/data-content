Wheel of Heaven is written in the first-person singular voice of one author and is open to contributors. Translations, corrections, infrastructure and discussion all benefit from many hands.

This list grows as people join. If you want to help, [Contributing](/contributing/) explains how, and the [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/wheelofheaven/discussions) are the place to start.
