Tobit is a Jewish tale of the Persian or Hellenistic period, preserved in Greek and placed among the Old Testament books in the Catholic and Orthodox canons. This translation covers **Tobit 12**, the chapter that *The Book Which Tells the Truth* quotes at 12:19–20:

- **12:1–10**: Tobit and Tobias offer the wages of the journey to their companion, who counsels prayer, fasting and alms
- **12:11–22**: he reveals that he is Raphael, "one of the seven holy angels": "I neither ate nor drank, but you were seeing a vision"; he goes up to the one who sent him, and they see him no more
