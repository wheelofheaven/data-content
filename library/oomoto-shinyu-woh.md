**Oomoto Shin'yu** (大本神諭, "Divine Revelations of Oomoto") is the founding scripture of the Japanese new religion Oomoto: automatic writings (*fudesaki*) produced through Deguchi Nao from 1892 onward. It is one of the two textual pillars of Oomoto, alongside Deguchi Onisaburō's *Reikai Monogatari*.

This translation covers the first oracle of 1892, which opens: "Throughout the three-thousand worlds, all together, the plum blossom opens; the age of Ushitora no Konjin has come to be." It announces the rebuilding and renewal of the world (*tatekae tatenaoshi*) and the return of the banished kami Ushitora no Konjin. *Kami* is left untranslated. A few readings that depend on the printed witness, such as 一同 "all together" in the first line, are flagged in the notes.
