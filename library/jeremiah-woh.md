Jeremiah (Hebrew *Yirmeyahu*) is the prophet of Jerusalem's last years before the Babylonian exile. This translation covers **Jeremiah 10**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites: "do not be dismayed at the signs of the skies"; the idols of the nations, cut from the forest and decked with silver and gold; and YHWH, the living God, who made the earth by his power. Verse 11 is in Aramaic, and the note says so. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
