Exodus (Hebrew *Shemot*, "Names") tells how Israel leaves Egypt and receives the covenant at Sinai. This translation covers the seven chapters that the Raëlian canon discusses verse by verse in *The Truth* and *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*:

- **Exodus 3**: the burning bush and the revelation of the name *ʾehyeh ʾăšer ʾehyeh*
- **Exodus 13**: the consecration of the firstborn and the pillar of cloud and fire
- **Exodus 14**: the crossing of the sea
- **Exodus 16**: the manna and the quail
- **Exodus 17**: water from the rock at Massah-Meribah and the battle with Amalek
- **Exodus 19**: YHWH's descent on Sinai in fire, smoke and the sound of the horn
- **Exodus 20**: the Ten Commandments and the altar law

The canon reads the pillar, the crossing, the manna and the Sinai descent as the work of the Elohim. The translation renders the Hebrew plainly; the notes set the canon's reading beside the traditional, naturalistic and source-critical readings. *ʾEhyeh ʾăšer ʾehyeh* is rendered "I am that I am", with other renderings in the notes; *rāṣaḥ* is "murder" and *ḥāmaḏ* "covet"; the commandments follow the Jewish numbering; and at 17:16 the Masoretic "throne" is kept, with the variant "banner" noted. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
