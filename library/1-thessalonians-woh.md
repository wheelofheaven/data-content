1 Thessalonians is Paul's earliest surviving letter, written to the church at Thessalonica around 50 CE. This translation covers **1 Thessalonians 5**, the chapter that *Let's Welcome the Extra-Terrestrials* cites:

- **5:1–11**: "the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night"; when they are saying "Peace and security", sudden destruction comes
- **5:12–28**: the closing exhortations and the blessing
