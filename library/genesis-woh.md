Genesis opens the Hebrew Bible with the creation of the world, the first humans and the garden, the flood, the Table of Nations and Babel, then follows Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph down to the descent into Egypt. This is a new English translation made from the Hebrew, printed beside the source so that each verse can be checked against it.

The translation keeps distinctions that most English versions smooth over. *Elohim* keeps its plural form; the three creation verbs *bara*, *asah* and *yatsar* stay distinct; the *raqia* of Genesis 1 is a hammered-out dome; *tselem* and *demut* in 1:26 describe physical resemblance; and Genesis 1:14–16 avoids naming the sun and moon. The verse notes explain each of these choices and set out the other readings scholars hold.

Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`. Select the marker to open the note.
