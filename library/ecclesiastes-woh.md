Ecclesiastes (Hebrew *Qohelet*, "the Assembler") is the reflection of a sage on what lasts under the sun. This translation covers **Ecclesiastes 3**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites: a time for everything under the skies, the eternity that God has set in the human heart, and the one fate of the sons of the human and the beasts.

At 3:21 the Masoretic vowels make the verse a statement where the consonants allow a question ("who knows whether the spirit of man goes upward?"); the note gives both readings. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
