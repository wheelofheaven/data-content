Numbers (Hebrew *Bemidbar*, "In the wilderness") follows Israel from Sinai to the plains of Moab. This translation covers the three chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **Numbers 18**: the charge of the priests and the Levites, and their portions, oblations, firstfruits and tithes
- **Numbers 21**: Chormah (Hormah), the serpent of bronze, the march around Moab, and the defeat of Sichon (Sihon) and Og
- **Numbers 33**: the stages of the wilderness journey, and the command to dispossess the land of Canaan

*Nəḥōšeṯ* is "bronze", not the ASV's "brass", and the notes give the wordplay of *nāḥāš* "serpent" and *nəḥōšeṯ* at 21:9. *Ḥērem* at 21:2–3 is "devote to destruction", so that the place name Chormah can be heard in the verb of its verse.
