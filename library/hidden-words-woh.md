**The Hidden Words** (*Kalimát-i-Maknúnih*) is a collection of short aphorisms written by Bahá'u'lláh around 1858, during his exile in Baghdad. Part I is in Arabic, Part II in Persian. Each aphorism opens with an address such as "O Son of Spirit" or "O ye that possess wit and ear", and the collection is among the most widely read works of the Bahá'í canon.

This is an independent English translation made directly from the Arabic and Persian and printed beside them. It is not affiliated with, and does not replace, the authorised translations of the Bahá'í World Centre. Wordplay in the original, such as the acrostic on the letters of *Bahá* in Part II, is kept in a literal surface rendering and explained in the notes.
