The book of Ezekiel opens with one of the most discussed visions in the Hebrew Bible: a storm wind out of the north, four living creatures (*chayyot*), wheels within wheels, a gleaming substance called *chashmal*, a dome above the creatures and a throne above the dome. This translation covers that vision (chapter 1), its return in chapter 10, where the glory (*kavod*) of YHWH leaves the Jerusalem temple and the creatures are identified as cherubim, and the valley of dry bones in chapter 37. In *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*, the Raëlian canon reads chapter 37 as a description of biological re-creation.

The translation also covers the other chapters that *The Book Which Tells the Truth* reads closely: the commission and the written scroll (2–3), with the ruach that lifts the prophet and the great rumbling sound behind him (3:12–14); the prophet carried to Jerusalem by a lock of hair (8); the glory leaving the city for the mountain to the east (11); and the Temple vision, in which a man like bronze with a measuring reed measures the gates and courts (40, 42), the glory returns by the east gate (43), and the Zadokite priests are set apart in linen garments (44).

The English stays close to the Hebrew. *Rûaḥ* is transliterated where it moves the prophet or is YHWH's own given, and is "spirit" for the human disposition (11:19 "a new spirit"); the Temple vision follows the room names used for Kings (porch, great hall, inner sanctuary), with *ʾêlām* as "vestibule". The verse notes cover the technical vocabulary of the vision (*chayyot*, *ofannim*, *chashmal*, *raqia*, *kavod*) and the main readings of the standard commentaries. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.

See also the [reference corpus of the Raëlian canon](/wiki/reference-corpus-of-the-raelian-canon/) and the [Genesis translation](/library/genesis-woh/).
