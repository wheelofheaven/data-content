The book of Ezekiel opens with one of the most discussed visions in the Hebrew Bible: a storm wind out of the north, four living creatures (*chayyot*), wheels within wheels, a gleaming substance called *chashmal*, a dome above the creatures and a throne above the dome. This translation covers that vision (chapter 1), its return in chapter 10, where the glory (*kavod*) of YHWH leaves the Jerusalem temple and the creatures are identified as cherubim, and the valley of dry bones in chapter 37. In *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*, the Raëlian canon reads chapter 37 as a description of biological re-creation.

The English stays close to the Hebrew. The verse notes cover the technical vocabulary of the vision (*chayyot*, *ofannim*, *chashmal*, *raqia*, *kavod*) and the main readings of the standard commentaries. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.

See also the [reference corpus of the Raëlian canon](/wiki/reference-corpus-of-the-raelian-canon/) and the [Genesis translation](/library/genesis-woh/).
