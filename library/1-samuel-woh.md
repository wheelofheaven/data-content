1 Samuel (Hebrew *Shemu'el*) tells of the last judge, the first king and the rise of David. This translation covers the four chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **1 Samuel 3**: YHWH calls Shemu'el (Samuel) in the night
- **1 Samuel 5**: the ark of the Elohim among the Pelishtim (Philistines), Dagon fallen before it, and the plague
- **1 Samuel 6**: the ark returned to Beit-Shemesh (Beth-shemesh)
- **1 Samuel 17**: David and Golyat (Goliath)

At 6:18 the line reads "the great stone" with some Hebrew manuscripts and the ancient versions: the Masoretic "meadow" cannot agree with the words around it. It is the one departure from the Masoretic text in these volumes, and the note sets out the evidence. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
