Deuteronomy (Hebrew *Devarim*, "Words") is Moses' farewell address on the plains of Moab. This translation covers **Deuteronomy 4**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites: the people heard a voice out of the fire at Horeb but "saw no likeness"; they are warned against making images and against bowing down to the sun, the moon and "all the host of the heavens"; and Moses sets apart three cities of refuge.

The physical sky is "skies" (4:11, 17, 19, 32) and YHWH's realm "heavens" (4:36, 39). At 4:32, "since the day that the Elohim created humankind upon the earth", *bārāʾ* is "create": the corpus keeps the Genesis rendering "shape" for Genesis alone.
