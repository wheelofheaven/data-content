This is a translation of the only three Qur'anic passages that the Raëlian canon cites by chapter and verse, in *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*: Sura 21:1–5, on the reckoning that has drawn near for humanity; Sura 54:1, "the Hour has drawn near and the moon has split"; and Sura 56:15–24, the description of paradise. It is not a translation of the Qur'an.

The translation starts from the oldest surviving manuscripts, which are written in the Hijazi script without the dots and vowel signs added in the following centuries, and uses the standard Cairo vocalization as a reference. The canon reads 54:1 as a literal event in the sky. The translation renders the Arabic plainly, and the notes set out the classical reading of the split moon as a past miracle, the modernist reading of it as a future sign, and the canon's reading. The notes also cover the *ḥūr ʿīn* of 56:22 (the "72 virgins" of popular polemic do not appear in the Qur'an), the scholarly debate on how stable the early text was, and the shared Semitic root of *Allāh* and *Elohim*. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note. The interlinear view shows the Arabic with its transliteration.
