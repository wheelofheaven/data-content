2 Kings continues the history of the two kingdoms from the death of Achazyahu (Ahaziah) to the fall of Jerusalem. This translation covers the four chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **2 Kings 1**: Achazyahu, Ba'al-Zevuv (Baal-zebub), and the fire on the captains of fifty
- **2 Kings 2**: Eliyahu (Elijah) taken up in the whirlwind to the heavens, the chariot and horses of fire, and Elisha's succession
- **2 Kings 4**: Elisha: the widow's oil, the Shunammite's son raised, the pot of stew, and the twenty loaves
- **2 Kings 17**: the fall of Shomron (Samaria) and the exile of Yisra'el (Israel)

*The Book Which Tells the Truth* reads the chariot and horses of fire as a craft of the Elohim taking off. The translation keeps the Hebrew literal: "a chariot of fire and horses of fire", and Eliyahu goes up "in the whirlwind", which is the vehicle in the Hebrew. The canon's reading sits beside the traditional and critical readings in the notes. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
