Jonah (Hebrew *Yonah*) is the story of a prophet who flees his commission. This translation covers **Jonah 2**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites: YHWH appoints a great fish to swallow Jonah, who prays from its belly and is cast out onto the dry land. Verse numbers follow the Hebrew, so the fish of the English 1:17 is verse 2:1 here. The notes give the rabbinic tradition of the two fish (Midrash Yonah, cited by Rashi). Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
