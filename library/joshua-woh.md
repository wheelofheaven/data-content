Joshua (Hebrew *Yehoshua*) tells of Israel's entry into Canaan. This translation covers the three chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **Joshua 5**: circumcision at Gilgal, the end of the manna, and the captain of the host of YHWH before Yericho (Jericho)
- **Joshua 6**: the fall of Yericho
- **Joshua 10**: the great stones from the skies and the sun standing still at Giv'on (Gibeon)

At 5:14 Yehoshua "fell on his face to the ground and bowed down": the narrative leaves the figure's status open, and the line does not decide it. *Ḥērem* is "devoted" and "devote to destruction" throughout, so the ban announced at 6:17 can be followed through the refrain of chapter 10.
