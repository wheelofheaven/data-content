2 Samuel (Hebrew *Shemu'el*) tells of David's reign. This translation covers **2 Samuel 6**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **6:1–11**: David sets out to bring up "the ark of the Elohim, which is called by the name, the name of YHWH of hosts, who is enthroned on the cherubim"; Uzzah takes hold of the ark and dies beside it
- **6:12–23**: the ark enters the city of David, David dances before YHWH, and Mikhal (Michal) despises him
