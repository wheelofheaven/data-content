Amos is the earliest of the writing prophets, a herdsman from Tekoa who prophesied against the northern kingdom. This translation covers **Amos 4**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites: the oracle against the "cows of Bashan", the plagues that end in the refrain "yet you have not returned to me, declares YHWH", and the hymn to "him who forms the mountains and creates the wind". *Bārāʾ* is "create" here, as everywhere outside Genesis.
