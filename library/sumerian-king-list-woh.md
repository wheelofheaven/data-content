The Sumerian King List opens with the formula that frames everything after it: **"After the kingship descended from heaven"**. Kingship begins at **Eridug** and passes through the cities before the flood (Bad-tibira, Larag, Zimbir, Curuppag), whose kings reign for tens of thousands of years, until **the flood swept over**. Kingship then descends from heaven a second time, and the list continues with the dynasties after the flood (Kish, Uruk with **Gilgamesh**, Ur and others down to Isin), the reigns shrinking toward historical lengths as the list approaches its own time.

For the Wheel of Heaven reading, the List is the Mesopotamian framework for the long lives before the flood and the reset the flood brings, read alongside the genealogies of Genesis 5–11. The line notes explain the lexical decisions, the manuscript variants and the different traditions of reign lengths, and point to parallels in the Hebrew Bible and other Mesopotamian texts.
