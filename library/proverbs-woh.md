Proverbs (Hebrew *Mishlei*) gathers the wisdom teaching attributed to Solomon. This translation covers the two chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **Proverbs 8**: Wisdom calls in the streets, and tells how she was beside YHWH before the earth was made
- **Proverbs 10**: the proverbs of Solomon begin: the righteous and the wicked

At 8:30 *ʾāmôn* is either "craftsman" or "nursling". The line follows the ASV; the note gives both, and records that the canon's French and English versions differ on it. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
