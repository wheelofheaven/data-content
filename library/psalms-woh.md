The Psalms (Hebrew *Tehillim*, "Praises") are Israel's book of song and prayer. This translation covers the five psalms that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **Psalm 8**: the majesty of YHWH's name, and a mortal made "a little less than Elohim"
- **Psalm 19**: the skies recount the glory of El, and the law of YHWH
- **Psalm 33**: YHWH looks down from the heavens on all the sons of the human
- **Psalm 139**: YHWH has searched me and known me
- **Psalm 147**: he counts the number of the stars

At 8:6 the Hebrew *mēʾĕlōhîm* has no article, and the line keeps it so: "a little less than Elohim". The Greek and the older English versions read "angels"; the notes give both. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
