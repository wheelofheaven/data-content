**Thánh Ngôn Hiệp Tuyển** ("Compilation of Divine Messages"), Volume One, gathers the dated séance messages received by the founders of Đạo Cao Đài from Christmas 1925 onward. They are attributed to the Supreme Being (Đức Chí Tôn) and other spirits, and written in the verse and prose forms typical of Caodai revelation. The church published Volume One in 1927–1928; it is a foundational text of the Third Universal Amnesty (*Tam Kỳ Phổ Độ*).

This translation covers the opening sequence of ten messages, from Christmas 1925 to April 1926, and keeps their poetic structure. The speaker, *Thầy*, is rendered "the Master", and the faithful are "my children". The notes record the passages where the single electronic witness may differ from the printed edition.
