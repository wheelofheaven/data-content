Leviticus (Hebrew *Vayikra*, "And he called") is the priestly law given at Sinai. This translation covers the two chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **Leviticus 17**: slaughter at the tent of meeting, and the prohibition of blood: "for the life of the flesh is in the blood"
- **Leviticus 25**: the Sabbath year, the jubilee, "the land is mine; for you are sojourners and residents with me", and the redemption of land and persons

The Hebrew *nefesh* is not rendered "soul": at 17:11 and 17:14 it is "life", and the canon's reading of the word is given in a note. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
