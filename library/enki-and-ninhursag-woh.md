*Enki and Ninhursag* is one of the foundational Sumerian myths of creation and paradise. It opens in Dilmun, a pure land where no animal preys on another and no one suffers illness or old age. Enki has Utu bring up fresh water from below and the land becomes fertile. A series of divine births follows. Enki then eats eight plants that have grown in the marsh and falls ill in eight parts of his body. Ninhursag curses him, is persuaded to return, and gives birth to eight healing deities, one for each ailing part.

About 91% of the composition survives. The notes compare it with the Eden story of Genesis 2–3: the paradise at the start, the forbidden plants, illness from forbidden food, healing, and the goddess Ninti, whose name can be read as "lady of the rib" or "lady of life".
