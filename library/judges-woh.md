Judges (Hebrew *Shofetim*) tells of the leaders of Israel between the conquest and the monarchy. This translation covers the three chapters that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **Judges 6**: the messenger of YHWH calls Gid'on (Gideon); fire rises from the rock and consumes the offering; the sign of the fleece
- **Judges 7**: the three hundred who lapped the water, the shofars, the jars and the torches, and the rout of Midyan (Midian)
- **Judges 13**: the messenger of YHWH announces the birth of Shimshon (Samson) and goes up in the flame of the altar

*Malʾaḵ YHWH* is "the messenger of YHWH", and the notes follow how the narrative moves between the messenger and YHWH himself. Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
