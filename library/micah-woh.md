Micah prophesied in Judah in the eighth century BCE. This translation covers **Micah 5**, the chapter that the Raëlian canon cites:

- **5:1–5**: "But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah", the ruler "whose goings forth are from of old", and "this one shall be peace"
- **5:6–14**: the remnant of Jacob among the peoples, like dew from YHWH and like a lion among the beasts

Verse numbers follow the Hebrew, so the Bethlehem oracle is 5:1 (5:2 in English Bibles). Verses with a note end in a bracketed marker `[ ]`; select it to open the note.
