Every dataset here is **machine-readable** (CSV and JSON), **CC0 public
domain**, and downloadable directly — no sign-up, no rate limits. The same
files are mirrored on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/wheelofheaven)
and [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/zarazinsfuss), and served from the
[static JSON API](https://api.wheelofheaven.world/v1/), which also exposes
the digitized source texts themselves as structured JSON with chapter and
verse references.
